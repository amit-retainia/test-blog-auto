Blog Publishing Pipeline

Sandbox for the automated publishing flow. Claude writes the post, commits it, Netlify deploys it. Nothing here is indexed by search engines.

How to publish

  1. Upload the hero image at /upload.html and copy the URL it gives you.
  2. In Claude, ask for the post. Paste the image URL when asked.
  3. Say “make it live”. Claude commits to GitHub.
  4. Netlify deploys in about a minute. Check /blog.

Rules Claude follows live in BLOG-PLAYBOOK.md in the repository.
